Frequently Asked Questions

Have a question regarding your dental visit in Ranchi? Here are answers to what patients ask most often.

Is Root Canal Treatment (RCT) painful at Hi Dent?

No! At Hi Dent Dental Clinic, Dr. Tapan Mandal utilizes modern computerized local anesthesia, electronic apex locators, and painless rotary technology. Most patients report feeling absolutely no pain during the procedure, only immediate relief from toothache.

Why is having 5 Specialist Doctors beneficial for patients?

Unlike generic dental clinics where a single general dentist performs every treatment, Hi Dent has dedicated super-specialists for Gum Diseases & Implants (Dr. Harish), Kids Dentistry (Dr. Moni Seema), Oral Surgery (Dr. Saurav), Root Canals (Dr. Tapan), and Braces/Aligners (Dr. Sparsh). You get specialized expertise under one roof.

Where is Hi Dent Dental Clinic located in Ranchi?

We are conveniently situated directly at Heritage Garden, Opposite Surendranath School in Deepatoli, Ranchi (Pincode: 834001). It is easily accessible with convenient roadside parking for two-wheelers and cars.

Can I book an appointment directly through WhatsApp?

Yes! You can either click the floating WhatsApp button or fill out the booking form on this page. It will automatically prepare your appointment details and send them directly to our clinic WhatsApp desk at +91 7004184115 for immediate confirmation.

What are the clinic's operating hours?

We are open Monday through Saturday from 9:30 AM to 8:30 PM, and on Sundays from 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM. Emergency dental assistance is also supported on call.
